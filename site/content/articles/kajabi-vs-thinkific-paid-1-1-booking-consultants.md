If you run paid group coaching with a 1:1 upsell, your tech stack has one job that matters more than the others: taking someone's money and then actually getting a time slot on your calendar, without you manually chasing emails. The course platform you pick determines whether that happens inside one tool or across two.

Here's what we could confirm from each platform's current pricing page, and where the gaps are.

## Thinkific: booking is a named feature, and it's not on the entry plan

Thinkific's pricing page is specific about this one. The Basic plan ($40/mo billed annually) covers courses, communities, digital products, and checkout, but there's no mention of calendars or live sessions anywhere in that tier's feature list.

The jump happens on Start, Thinkific's "Most Popular" plan at $82/mo billed annually. Its feature list includes, verbatim: "Live cohorts and coaching: Unlimited Zoom sessions, bookable calendars, and attendance tracking." That's the line that matters for a consultant selling 1:1 upsells. Start also adds bundles, upsells, and payment plans in the same tier, which is relevant because an upsell flow needs both the booking mechanism and the pricing mechanism to exist together.

So on Thinkific, the honest answer is: native bookable calendars start at $82/mo (annual billing), not on the $40 entry plan. If you're on Basic, you don't have it, and you'd need a separate booking tool bolted on regardless of what else Basic includes.

Grow ($164/mo annual) and the custom enterprise tier add more communities, B2B invoicing, API access, and support, but the booking feature itself doesn't change between Start and Grow based on what's listed. The calendar capability shows up once, at Start, and carries forward.

## Kajabi: we couldn't confirm the details this time

This is where we have to be straight with you instead of guessing. The Kajabi pricing page fetch for this article didn't return usable plan content, just the page title. That means we can't tell you which Kajabi tier includes native calendar or booking functionality, what it's called, or how it compares to Thinkific's Start-tier feature.

Kajabi has historically marketed calendar and booking capability as part of its offer-building tools, but we're not going to restate that from memory as if it were verified against current pricing. If you're actively deciding between the two platforms on this specific feature, pull up kajabi.com/pricing yourself and check which tier lists calendar or scheduling functionality before you commit. We'll revisit this comparison once we can fetch the real tier breakdown.

## What this means if you're running paid group coaching with a 1:1 upsell

The pattern that actually matters for your business model isn't "which platform has booking," it's "does booking live in the same place as checkout." When a student buys your course or group program and you want to immediately sell them a 1:1 call, every extra step between "they paid" and "they have a slot" is a point where they close the tab.

On Thinkific, that means Start tier or above if you want the native path. If you're running Basic for cost reasons, you're already going to need Calendly, Acuity, or a similar scheduler connected to your checkout flow, and you should budget for that integration working cleanly rather than assuming it will.

On Kajabi, we can't tell you the tier yet. Treat any claim you see elsewhere about which plan unlocks it with the same skepticism we're applying here until you've checked the current pricing page.

## The separate-tool fallback, either way

Even with native bookable calendars, a lot of consultants still end up layering Calendly or Acuity on top for one reason: timezone handling and buffer rules across a client base that isn't all in the same region. Native platform calendars tend to cover the basics (connect your availability, let someone grab a slot after paying) but don't always match a dedicated scheduler's handling of things like round-robin assignment if you bring on a second coach, or buffer times between back-to-back calls.

If your 1:1 upsell is simple, one coach, one calendar, same timezone cluster of clients, the native option on whichever platform you're already paying for is probably enough and saves you a second monthly bill. If you're scaling past that, the separate tool earns its keep regardless of which course platform you're on.
